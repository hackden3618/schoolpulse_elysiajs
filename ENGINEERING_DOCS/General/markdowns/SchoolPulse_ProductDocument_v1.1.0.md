I created SchoolPulse because I believe schools deserve better systems for the work they already carry every day.

## What SchoolPulse Is

SchoolPulse is a cloud-based school management platform that brings student records, fee management, attendance, academics, parent communication, and reporting into one connected system.

It is designed for Kenyan schools that want to move beyond paper registers, ledger books, disconnected spreadsheets, and informal communication channels. It gives school leaders a dependable operating view of what is happening across the school.

SchoolPulse helps answer the questions school leaders ask every day:

- Which students are present, absent, or late today?
- Which parents have been notified?
- Which students have outstanding balances?
- Which payments have been received?
- Which receipts were issued?
- Which classes need attention?
- Which students are academically or operationally at risk?
- What can the principal see right now without calling several offices?

Admissions staff register students and guardians. Academic leaders configure classes, streams, subjects, terms, and assessments. Teachers mark attendance and enter results. Bursars manage invoices, payments, receipts, and balances. Principals monitor the school from a daily dashboard. Parents receive timely updates and can view the information that matters to them.

## Keeping Learners in Class Through Better Fee Communication

One of the most painful school experiences is when a student is interrupted because of a fee issue the parent could have addressed earlier.

SchoolPulse helps schools handle this more professionally.

When a student has an outstanding balance, the school can send clear fee reminders to the guardian before the issue reaches the gate or classroom. The parent receives the amount due, the student details, and payment instructions. When payment is recorded, SchoolPulse generates a receipt and updates the student account.

This gives the school a better record, gives the parent earlier notice, and helps prevent unnecessary disruption to learning.

SchoolPulse respects each school's fee policy. It does not make decisions for the school. It gives the school better information, earlier communication, and proper records.

## Attendance That Parents Can Trust

Attendance is valuable only when it is timely and reliable.

With SchoolPulse, attendance records are tied to the correct class, date, session, and authorized staff member. When a student is marked absent or late, the school can notify the guardian promptly.

This helps schools improve student accountability, parent communication, and daily oversight.

## Finance With Receipts and Accountability

SchoolPulse treats school finance records with the seriousness they deserve.

Payments, receipts, invoices, balances, and statements are recorded in a structured way. Confirmed financial records are protected from casual editing. Where a correction is needed, the system supports proper reversal and audit history instead of simply deleting the original record.

This protects the school, the bursar, the parent, and the student.

## Academic Visibility Before It Is Too Late

Academic progress should not only be discovered at the end of term.

SchoolPulse helps schools record assessments, publish results, and generate report cards in a structured way. Parents and school leaders can understand academic performance earlier, giving room for support before the problem grows.
